Admittedly, for many tasks, we don't need a system as sophisticated as a human being. We also don't need to fully comprehend the mechanisms of human intelligence to develop a system with remarkable generalization capabilities. However, these systems are still not generalist agents, no matter what the PR says. <br/> My research interest started from general object understanding, moved to human-centric manipulation, and now centers on the safety of human-robot interaction &mdash; human motion mechanisms, omni-modal perception, and high-frequency force control. Many ongoing projects in these newer areas are not reflected in the paper list yet. </p>
