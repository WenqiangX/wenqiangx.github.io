I am an Assistant Professor at the [Institute of Trustworthy Embodied AI](https://teai.fudan.edu.cn), Fudan University, leading [交叉学科智能系统设计与实现实验室 (Design and Realization of Interdisciplinary Intelligent System Lab, DrXs Lab)](https://drxslab.github.io). We are recruiting PhD students, master's students, interns and postdocs for research. If you are interested, please check out more details at [Group Page](https://drxslab.github.io).

Before joining Fudan, I was a Research Fellow at NUS, advised by [**Prof. Shao Lin**](https://linsats.github.io/), and an Assistant Research Fellow at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. My PhD and MS were completed at the School of SEIEE, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, under the supervision of **Prof. Cewu Lu**. Prior to this, I earned my bachelor's degree from the School of the Gifted Young at the University of Science and Technology of China.

My research interest centers on the **safety of robot interaction**: how robots can work in physical contact with people/objects without putting them at risk. I approach it along four directions — **human motion mechanisms** (musculoskeletal modeling and biomechanically grounded motion understanding), **omni-modal perception & high-frequency force control** (fusing vision, touch, force and proprioception into a single picture of contact), **safe interaction policy** (during navigation, locomotion and manipulation), and the design of **novel embodiments and sensors** that make safe physical interaction a property of the hardware itself.
