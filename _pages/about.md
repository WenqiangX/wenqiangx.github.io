I am an Assistant Professor at the [Institute of Trustworthy Embodied AI](https://teai.fudan.edu.cn), Fudan University. Before joining Fudan, I was a Research Fellow at NUS, advised by [Prof. Shao Lin](https://linsats.github.io/), and an Assistant Research Fellow at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. My PhD and MS were completed at the School of SEIEE, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, under the supervision of **Prof. Cewu Lu**. Prior to this, I earned my bachelor's degree from the School of the Gifted Young at the University of Science and Technology of China.

My research interest centers on the **safety of human-robot interaction**: how robots can work in physical contact with people without putting them at risk. I approach it along four directions — **human motion mechanisms** (musculoskeletal modeling and biomechanically grounded motion understanding), **omni-modal perception** (fusing vision, touch, force and proprioception into a single picture of contact), **high-frequency force control**, and the design of **novel embodiments and sensors** that make safe physical interaction a property of the hardware itself.

I am recruiting PhD students, master's students, interns and postdocs who want to work on these problems. Feel free to reach out at <a href="mailto:{{ site.email | encode_email }}">wenqiangxu [at] fudan.edu.cn</a>.
